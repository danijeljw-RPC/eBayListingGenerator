Condition Guide
I use these terms consistently across my listings, based on cosmetic condition only (not performance), unless stated otherwise.
  • Excellent - Looks near-new. No obvious marks; at most tiny hairline signs of handling.
  • Very Good - Clean overall. If any wear exists, it's minor and hard to notice at normal viewing distance.
  • Good - Visible wear such as light scratches/scuffs, small nicks, or minor shine on touch areas. (e.g. small paint chips or marks around logos, depending on severity)
  • Fair - Obvious cosmetic wear: heavier scratches, multiple chips, dents, or noticeable paint loss. Fully functional but clearly used.
  • For Parts / Not Working - Sold as-is for parts or repair. Not fully functional, incomplete, or untested. Cosmetic condition may vary significantly. No returns.
